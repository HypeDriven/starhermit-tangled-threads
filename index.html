How to play

Goal. Route every colored strand across the board so its tip reaches the spool of the same color and shape, then reel it in.
No crossings. Strands may never share or cross a cell. Occupied cells are blocked for every other strand.
Routing. Tap a strand to select it, then tap or drag through empty neighboring cells to extend it. Tap the cell behind the tip (or press U) to pull back.
Reeling. When the tip rests on its matching spool, tap the spool or press R to reel the strand in. Reel all strands to finish the board.
Scoring. Completion (up to 1000) + efficiency vs par (up to 500) + hint independence (up to 200) + discipline (up to 100). Fewer moves and zero hints score higher.
Keyboard. Tab/←→↑↓ move among cells · Enter/Space select or extend · R reel · U undo · H hint · Esc pause · C reset camera.
